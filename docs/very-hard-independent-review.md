## Tactical proof soundness findings

No confirmed remaining false-positive tactical certificate was found in this bounded review.

1. **Independent engine boundary.** The general engine neither subclasses `AIHard` nor reuses its evaluator. The tactical module imports the rules, not either AI, Board or UI. A large general-search score is never converted into a public proven-win status.
2. **Legal terminal geometry.** A black completion must form a maximal exact five in at least one axis; white/custom targets permit at least N. A positive black exact-five completion does not need a later double-three/four veto because the rule contract gives the exact five priority across axes. Opening coordinates are separately applied.
3. **All-board counterwins first.** Every defender AND node checks for an immediate legal defender win before using an endpoint shortcut. The preceding forced defense’s counter-four is checked again at the next attacker node. A remote counterwin is not discarded by proximity or a candidate cap.
4. **Winning-square partition is sound within the placement model.** Given one immediate attacker completion, every nonwinning defender placement outside that square leaves the completing attacker-stone line intact. The sole legal block is searched as an actual new board, including any counterthreat it makes. If the blocking square is forbidden for black, it is not treated as a defense. With two distinct completions, one defender stone cannot occupy both. An opponent stone elsewhere cannot turn the surviving all-attacker line into an overline or remove its exact five; black’s other-axis fouls cannot invalidate that terminal winning placement.
5. **Three-only VCT uses real AND coverage.** With no immediate attacker completion, the solver enumerates every legal defender placement and a conservative pass. It does not assume that only a three extension point can defend. Every branch must have a completed attacker certificate; one unknown/refutation discards the entire proposed win. This correctly includes remote endpoint defenses, counter-fours and tempo-changing replies.
6. **Pass/opening treatment is conservative and explicit.** A modeled defender pass leaves placement count unchanged. The partition separately checks pass completion availability instead of borrowing the count after a placement. The app does not implement full tournament pass/draw/claim procedure; the solver’s stronger defensive option is conservative for its positive placement-strategy certificates.
7. **UNKNOWN is not LOSS.** Restricted attacker candidates can miss quiet wins. Depth/node/time exhaustion, refuted nominated roots and an unproved general evaluation remain UNKNOWN. The solver’s public loss is reserved for an already terminal opponent position; the general root separately certifies elementary unavoidable immediate losses. General beam-search mate-like values remain estimates.
8. **A strategy tree, not merely a cooperative PV.** The tactical result includes every all-defense child or an explicit winning-square coverage partition. Tests replay the tree and independently reconstruct immediate geometry; they do not just replay the displayed PV. Those tests still use the shared Rules implementation for legality, so they are not an independent external rules oracle.
9. **Mutation and memo correctness.** Tactical input is copied once. Root, defender and recursive-rule hypothetical placements restore through `finally`. Only completed winning subtrees are memoized, keyed by complete immutable board tuples, attacker, placement count and depth. No unchecked Zobrist collision can become a tactical proof. Parent cancellation exceptions propagate; local resource interruption yields UNKNOWN without a partial certificate.

The hand VCF four-three, forbidden-point trap, remote counterwin and white cross VCT arguments were inspected geometrically as well as read in the test expectations. In particular, the white cross’s row/off-row partition leaves the perpendicular six-cell straight-four corridor untouched; black’s tiny stone count precludes a counterwin. This is a useful shallow composed proof, not a reachable tournament record or evidence of deep practical VCT capability.

## General-search mechanics

- Terminal negamax scores are side-to-move relative: the previous mover’s win returns `-MATE + ply`; negation makes that favorable to its parent. An own immediate win is checked before required defense. Nonterminal scores are clamped to ±1,000,000, well inside the ±1,000,000,000 terminal scale.
- Incremental evaluation updates every N-cell window and each relevant before/after endpoint; make/unmake also updates piece counts, occupied set, immediate-threat windows and Zobrist hash. The focused rebuild tests cover targets 3–7 and nested exception unwinding. No missing affected-endpoint update was found by source inspection.
- General quiet candidates are intentionally incomplete. They use attack/defense window structure and multiple axes, not just neighbor count. Immediate winning and blocking squares bypass the quiet beam cap. Legal filtering happens before make.
- Quiescence-like forced-defense continuation runs beyond depth zero, but has a hard negative-depth horizon. Longer forcing chains may therefore become estimates; that is a strength/horizon limitation, not a tactical proof. No such estimate is promoted to a certificate.
- TT keys include board hash, side and placement count; the table is cleared each root because scores contain root-relative mate distance. Bound types use the original alpha/beta window. Terminal checks run before lookup, so different valid last-move metadata cannot reuse a terminal value as a quiet value. A 64-bit collision could still affect a heuristic estimate, unlike the exact tactical memo.
- Only fully completed iterations replace the legal fallback. Timeout/cancel within a move unwinds the position scope. Progress observer exceptions are contained by logging rather than corrupting the search state.
- Empty/full/no-legal-move roots return a legal fallback or None without inventing a winning certificate. Historical invalid boards are not generally reconstructed or adjudicated beyond the new all-board terminal scan.

## UI / compatibility / resource boundary

- Four difficulties have distinct internal values, including `very_hard`, and the display map uses `Very Hard`. The factory keeps Hard’s depth2/1.5s default; VH defaults to 5s. One existing shared AI-difficulty setting applies to both sides in AI-vs-AI, as before; the app does not newly offer separate per-color difficulty selectors.
- Worker requests carry a detached board and a token. Polling compares size, target, exact grid bytes, last move, player and count before consuming a result. Cancel/reset/history/menu/quit cannot apply an old result to a new position.
- Exhaustive recursive black forbidden overlays and research evaluation now run in the detached worker. Main rendering computes only opening geometry and cached visual heuristics. Incomplete overlays do not invent legality. Research remains explicitly **Hard static**, not VH proof/evaluation.
- A residual responsiveness boundary was reported to the parent: final selected-black-move revalidation and actual placement called one unbounded full rule query on the main thread. The parent subsequently added `Board.validation_budget_sec`, set to **0.012s** on the live game board. The rules callback raises `RuleValidationTimeout` on exhaustion; the assignment into the legality cache does not occur. Adapter polling drops the unapplied move with an explicit incomplete-validation error; human placement shows an incomplete-adjudication telop and leaves the board unchanged. Source inspection confirms the timeout is not cached as a foul or converted into a legal verdict. A no-cache-on-timeout regression was added by the parent, but not rerun during the exclusive match batch. **Limitation:** an exceptionally complex legal black move may remain unapplied/unadjudicated rather than freeze the UI; the current implementation does not automatically resume that exact pending move’s rule proof in a background worker. The engine’s initial verified fallback still intentionally runs without its own search deadline, within the process wall cap.
- TT entries, geometry caches, rule memo and tactical node/depth counts are bounded. At a follow-up, the parent added [Windows process budgets](../worker_limits.py) and the adapter attaches a default **512MiB per-process committed-memory Job Object cap in unfrozen Python (1024MiB startup allowance in frozen builds)**, with kill-on-job-close and pre-import single-thread BLAS environment settings. Source inspection confirms this applies to the supplied child, checks native handle/PID identity, owns cleanup, and explicitly reports **no native cap on unsupported non-Windows platforms**. Attachment occurs immediately after Popen, not as a suspended-before-execution launch; there is a small unbudgeted startup race. The parent reports roughly 39MB RSS / 28MB commit in one sample and a 64MiB-capped 192MiB allocation failing with MemoryError. These measurements were not independently rerun by this reviewer and are not universal worst-case bounds.
- SDL dummy/process tests are valuable, but this reviewer did not personally inspect a native Pygame window, run the frozen executable or verify all transitions in a packaged distribution. Those remain explicit parent integration gates.

## Remaining evidence and acceptance gates

1. The parent initially reported 366 passing tests, a same-1.5s tactical comparison and eight short-budget wins. A later parent-only evidence update reports VH equal-1.5s versus improved Hard **20W/4L**, versus old Hard **22W/2L**, and product-default **16W/0L**, over a small paired opening corpus (eight unique openings in the stated equal-time improved-Hard comparison). Those reports were context, **not** independently rerun review-certified results. Repeated deterministic openings and paired colors are not independent Bernoulli samples; report the corpus and intervals with that dependence, not a large independent sample claim.
2. Require final serial matched/color-swapped comparison artifacts, with tactical correctness/errors, timeouts, drawn/capped games and appropriately qualified confidence intervals. The parent’s conservative Hard-versus-old-Hard comparison remained in progress at the update and did **not** establish no-regression (interim 5W/6L/4 capped/censored). That interim result must not be treated as final or hidden behind the promising VH scores. Stronger claims still need broader independent reachable/published positions.
3. Require measured peak-memory behavior and tests for the final selected-move 12ms UNKNOWN/unapplied policy above, plus native UI and packaged-build checks or an explicit unexecuted limitation. The policy's source fix is reviewed; no automatic deferred adjudication is promised.
4. Rule/tactical corpus has independently reasoned sparse compositions but few published reachable histories and little deep VCT diversity. No external strong-engine or federation adjudicator has certified all positions.
5. Full competitive opening, clocks, claims, missed-foul history and tournament pass rules remain out of scope. Product docs must say placement-rule core plus the existing simplified opening, not full competitive Renju protocol.
6. Architecture currently lists broad preparatory concepts such as center control and future attack motifs; the implemented general value is window potential plus multi-axis candidate ordering, not a separately validated semantic recognizer for every named Renju concept. Do not advertise those names as independently measured capabilities.
