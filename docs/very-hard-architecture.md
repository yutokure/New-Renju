# Very Hard architecture (Phase B implementation contract)

## Separation

`AIHard` stays a conservative depth-2 minimax engine. `AIVeryHard` does not inherit from it and does not reuse its evaluation. Shared components are placement rules, process lifecycle, and the public find_move/progress interface only. The default VH budget is 5s and caller-configurable.

## Rule boundary

`renju_rules.Rules` is independent from evaluation/search and operates on a flat mutable 0/1/2 list with guarded hypothetical placements. Standard validation is 15×15 connect-five: exact black five, white >=5, exact-five precedence, overline, distinct four identities including broken/same-axis shapes, recursive real-three legal nonwinning straight-four witnesses. Non-five targets are symmetric custom >=N without fouls. The existing restricted first-three-placement policy is a separately switchable legacy opening, not a tournament protocol. Other board sizes with five are explicitly a custom extension, not RIF board-size compliance.

## Search result semantics

- `PROVEN_WIN`: a legal attacker strategy exists and every required legal defender response/counterwin has been covered by a sound terminal or AND-node argument.
- `PROVEN_LOSS`: a certified opponent winning strategy/terminal loss, not merely failure to find our attack.
- `UNKNOWN`: deadline, node/depth/candidate budget, or no positive certificate found. An incomplete restricted tactical search never becomes a loss proof.

General search estimates are separate from proof states. A heuristic alpha-beta value near a win is not exported as a tactical certificate.

## Tactical proof search

Use an attacker OR / defender AND search, with VCF first and optional VCT extension. Attacker candidate filtering may miss winning attacks but cannot make an explored false win sound. Defender pruning requires a justified coverage argument:

1. Any defender immediate legal win refutes the attack, regardless of proximity.
2. With one immediate attacker winning square, all nonwinning defender replies outside it lose next ply. Examine the legal blocking square and counterplay it creates. If the square is forbidden for the defender, it is not a defense.
3. Two distinct legal immediate attacker winning squares, with no defender immediate win, certify a win: one defender stone cannot occupy both; exact-five priority makes a later black winning placement legal.
4. With only a three and no immediate winning square, enumerate **all** legal defender placements (and a modeled defensive pass) rather than assuming the extension square is the only defense. Counter-fours and remote endpoint blocks are included.
5. A terminal certificate is rule-checked. Make/unmake always restores state on exceptions/deadline.

The composed positions and hand proofs in `rules-research.md` are independent test expectations, not answers generated by this engine.

## General search

A separate flat-position engine uses negamax/alpha-beta, iterative deepening, bounded Zobrist transpositions, principal-variation move ordering, and tactical-first window-based candidate ranking. Forced immediate wins/blocks are never discarded for a candidate cap. Quiet general candidates can be restricted for practicality, explicitly yielding heuristic (not proven) values. Completed iterations replace the safe legal fallback; partial iterations cannot overwrite it.

Evaluation is side-to-move relative and nonterminal values are strictly below terminal magnitudes. It combines immediate completion potential, open/broken near-winning lines, multi-axis preparation, defensive urgency and center control; it does not interpret a large sum as proof. Distance-to-terminal rewards quicker wins/slower losses only in explored terminal lines.

## Performance and operational limits

Profile before adopting optimization. Start with flat lists and precomputed line/window geometry, incremental hash, make/unmake and bounded caches/TT. No initial native-language rewrite. Measure time/nodes/solved tactical problems and matched game results, not feature count.

Deadline/cancel checks cover rule callbacks, candidate loops, AND/OR recursion and negamax. The existing process adapter independently enforces wall-clock budget+startup grace, cancels on transitions, and validates the exact position token before applying a move. The UI must never execute VH search synchronously.

## Opening data

The existing JSON is a name-display list of three-move patterns, not a validated best-move book. Do not treat labels as best-play recommendations. Without a separately validated and rules-tagged move book, VH uses normal search and continues out of book.

## Acceptance

Implementation tests, finite rule fixtures and a handful of wins are not sufficient for a stronger-than-Hard claim. Report matched color-swapped games at equal budget and defaults, tactical accuracy, errors/timeouts and confidence intervals. Any known critical rule error or statistically insufficient strength evidence keeps overall status **CHANGES REQUESTED**.
